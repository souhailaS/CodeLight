## Say what you noticed

With the cursor in a highlight, press **cmd alt M** on macOS, or **ctrl K ctrl G** elsewhere. A comment thread opens right there in the editor.

Write as much as you like, reply to whoever wrote the note before you, and edit or delete your own notes in place. A note carries your GitHub account when you are signed in, and otherwise the name git knows you by, so nothing here waits on a sign in.
